01About

I am a software developer with more than fifteen years of experience, most of it spent deep in the backend: .NET, distributed systems and the high-traffic platforms behind betting in Türkiye. I enjoy complex problems and layered architectures.

Over the last year my work has taken a decisive turn. I now spend most of my time on agentic coding — writing tools for AI agents, building AI servers, and taking an active role in my company's AI journey.

The goal is practical: AI solutions that earn their place in monitoring, in coding and in performance work — built by someone who knows what production actually demands.

“Fifteen years taught me how systems fail. The last one taught me how agents can help keep them running.”
